What implementation changes

Grouped by the file that owns it. Anything not listed is unchanged on purpose.

src/ui/hud.tstop bar and deck
Top bar keeps cash and livesDrop the era rail from renderTop — it moves to its own square. The .stat cells and the ribbon are otherwise unchanged.
Wave overview joins the ribbonThe grouped contact chips and toughest-ehp line from renderNextContact move into the top bar in versus, so the wave preview stays readable while a station is selected. Today it renders only when the detail slot is empty, which it never is once you click a tower.
Send-wave button sits with the ribbonThe intermission button from renderSend moves beside the progress bar, carrying the rush bonus (timer × BALANCE.rushBonusPerSecond, 2.5/s). Spawning and done stay flat readouts.
The deck stops being one bottom panelFour docks instead: build column at the left edge, sortie deck under the board, detail column at the right, era square bottom-left. renderDeck emits the same sections; the move is in styles.css.
Build slots become icon-onlyIn renderSlots, keep kbd top-left and .corner top-right; drop the .name footer and the title blurb. Name, blurb and the derived mechanics() figures move into a hover flyout — title never fires on touch, which is the bug this closes.
Locked slots unchangedGate named in the corner, glyph dimmed. At era II that is Filament alone (era 3).
Inspector shows damage dealtThe figure already exists as a title on .blurb; in the taller right column it becomes text — "9 kills · 2.0k damage dealt".
Sortie cards gain name and hullCards are glyph plus cost today. In the wider deck they carry the contact name and its hull at the receiver’s wave, which is the number the send is judged on. The lane toggle stays a mode, unchanged.
src/ui/raceHud.tsthe opponent column
Minimap becomes a panelThe bare canvas gains a stats column — lives, wave, era, sent-you, and the standing render() already computes as ahead / behind / level. Same 2Hz blob, same TowerPin list, no new wire traffic.
Label states what it is"What they’ve built — their contacts are not shared", so a static board never reads as a stalled one.
Feed becomes a horizontal exchangeTwo rows — you, them — of contact glyphs oldest to newest with a lane tag, replacing toast-only history. Needs a small client-side ring buffer of sorties sent and received; nothing new crosses the wire, since both events already arrive.
Toasts stayincoming, peerConn and the wave-cleared toast are unchanged. The strip is the record; the toast is still the alarm.
src/render/stationShape.tsone mark redrawn
Singularity is a gravity wellReplace the three arcs with a sampled inward spiral — r 3.2 to 13.2 over 3.4π, 32 segments — plus a filled core at r 2.1. The old arcs read as a clock rather than as something that pulls. Stays inside the r 0.16–0.32 annulus the file requires, so nothing else moves.
src/ui/icons.ts · src/render/textures.tsthe centre orb
Drop the hub discRemove the r=4 centre circle from stationIcon and the tier-grown hub from the bake. Investment reads positionally on the collar; a brighter core said the same thing twice and blurred the mark.
Collar and dials unchangedcollarDial, pathDial and tierPips ship as they are — the design only draws them larger.
src/render/mapLayer.ts · theme.tsbackground and map styling
The page ground is the lobby’s skyBehind the letterboxed board, the match paints the same static star field the lobby uses — nebula gradients, two star layers, no animation. This is the single largest reason the current screen feels emptier than the lobby: outside the board it is flat black. Static by design; nothing here costs a frame.
The board keeps its own fieldInside the frame the field stays THEME.fields[…].bg with its own nebulae, so board and page are two related grounds rather than one flat one. The letterbox that fitCanvas already produces is where the page ground shows through — it should read as space around the sector, not as a missing background.
Grid fades, and never where you buildDrawn with GRID_MASK (rx 0.9, ry 0.95, inner 0.42, outer 1.02) so the board has a centre of gravity, with GRID_MASK_FLOOR 0.34 holding on every buildable tile — the invariant tools/check.ts already asserts. Front Line has buildable corners, so this is load-bearing on this map specifically.
Scenery is drawn, not impliedThe x tiles at cols 13–19 are the measured plug and flanges that make committing to one lane beat hedging by 3.25×. They read as blocked terrain — inset, darker, edged — because a player who cannot see them cannot see why the board is shaped like this.
Both lanes glow, both entrances are markedCrest and Trough each get the route glow, the thin bright centreline and the dashed travel ticks. Both midfield spawns are marked on the left edge and the core at the right, since on Front Line the left edge is where the neutral wave and their sorties arrive.
Lane names on the boardCrest and Trough are labelled once each, near their entrances, in muted type clear of the road. The sortie deck’s lane toggle uses the same two words, so picking a lane and watching one are the same vocabulary.
src/ui/styles.cssthe layout itself
New grid, four docksBuild column 88px left, board fitted to its own aspect, sortie deck beneath it, detail column 488px right running full height, era square 88×92 bottom-left. Nothing overlaps the board at any size — the reason this beats floating the HUD over it.
Panels near-opaqueA translucent panel over the star field lets background elements read as controls. Panel grounds go to rgba(10,11,20,.93).
Hover flyoutNew rule for the build column, opening to the right of the column and never over the board.
no changedeliberately
The simulationNothing in sim/ moves. Costs, tiers, sortie pricing and targeting are read through what already exists — upgradeCost, tierCeiling, sortieCost, effectiveInterval, sellValue.
The mapmaps/versus.ts is drawn as authored: 26×15, Crest and Trough, the five-tile merge, the measured plug and flanges.
TargetingFour modes with the active one labelled — exactly as renderInspector already does it.
Still open
  • Compact and touch layouts: the build column and era square are the natural bottom sheet, but the sortie deck under the board needs a decision at phone height.
  • How deep the exchange strip runs before it fades or scrolls.
  • Whether the spiral Singularity mark also replaces the slow indicator drawn on contacts, which currently owns the ring shape.
